# CRT1.0.0 Business Retrospective

## What did we do well?

- Project team communication - positive, informative and timely.
- Project teams flexibility to listening to ideas and concerns and adapting them.
- Team did a great job in urgency of the project and stuck to the timeline, good to have the project running in first quarter of this fiscal.
- Good interaction for the requirements, the spatial and additional enhancements were really good to have.
- Sets a good standard for IPS with the user's will be have the ability to use this for some time before IPS is online.
- Sprint review frequency and information provided was good.
- Active participation from business area was extremely valuable for the project team.

## What should we have done better?

- Blue sky conversation - to expand on various possibilities/brain-storm session to see what is possible overall as the way forward for the product.
- Some of the meetings tend to get heavy on technical terminology, may need to work on finding that balance.
