# Non-Functional Requirements

**Storage:** Issues relating to storage should have little impact on the performance of the proposed Solution.

**Legal, Regulatory and Policy Constraints:** Solution shall comply with Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure Legal, Regulatory and Policy Constraints including the following:

- **FOIPPA Compliance:**
  - Solution shall comply with the Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act of British Columbia (FOIPPA), including requirements around storage and disposition of personal information.
  - Solution shall provide the ability to remove personal information about customers while retaining relevant trip data.
- **MOTI Compliance:**
  - Solution shall comply with all Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure IMB policies and procedures.
- **Records Management Compliance:**
  - Solution shall comply with all Ministry of British Columbia IMB retention schedules requirements, archiving requirements and deletion requirements.
  - Solution shall support automated archiving and/or deletion of data based on pre-defined data rules as outlined in the Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure IMB policies and procedures.
  - Solution shall provide an authorized user with a summary of impacted records for review prior to archiving or deleting them.

**Number of concurrent users:**

For phase 1 of the project there could be the following concurrent users:
- MoTI Admin ~ 25, across various regions
